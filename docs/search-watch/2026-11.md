## 2. Audit findings

### Site wide

- **robots.ts:** fine. All crawlers are allowed, including Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot. Only the trap, `?_rsc=` and `/api/` are disallowed. Nothing in this window calls for a change.
- **sitemap.ts:** fine. Practice `lastmod` uses `contentUpdatedAt`. Blog posts use their publish date. Learn pages use `lastUpdated`.
- **llms.txt route:** valid under v2. Optional v2 extras (a `Link: </llms.txt>; rel="describedby"` header) aren't worth doing until an AI tool is shown to use them.
- **Article JSON-LD (`src/components/ArticleDetail.tsx:214`):** the house author is `{"@type":"Organization","name":"Open Air Allergy Network"}` with no `url` and no link to the homepage Organization. Google's Article structured data docs recommend `author.url` so the author can be identified. Small fix (fix 3). Everything else is fine: `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `image`, FAQPage, and the credited-author and MedicalWebPage paths.
- **`dateModified` comes from `lastUpdated` (`@updatedAt`).** Any row write moves it. `src/lib/sync.ts:617` already writes only on a real credit change, and `publish-article.ts` is the only other writer, so it's honest today. Keep it that way: any new script that touches article rows will move the visible "Last reviewed" date on Learn pages.
- **Homepage Organization JSON-LD (`src/app/(site)/page.tsx:35`):** fine (logo, sameAs, contactPoint). It has no `@id`. Adding one (`{SITE_URL}/#organization`) lets fix 3 point at it.
- **About page content disclosure:** says "We use AI tools to help research and draft. Every article is then checked against its sources and reviewed by an editor." It doesn't mention images, and at least one hero (puffy eyes) is AI generated (fix 4).
- **docs/metadata-rules.md:** two stale spots. "Last audited 2026-09-18" predates about 20 articles and the 10-30 title changes. The "ILIT needs allergy context" section (line 155) still gives the old state page H1, "ILIT Allergy Treatment Providers in {State}", but the table at line 75 shows the 2026-10-30 "ILIT Allergists in {State}". It's a doc fix only.
- **Live checks (browser pane):** the dog and puffy eyes pages render the right title, description, self canonical and `index, follow, max-image-preview:large`, with Article and FAQPage JSON-LD present. The hero alt text matches the photo on both.

## 3. Top fixes, ranked

1. **Restore the ILIT evidence caveat on the dog allergy article.** Add one line to the body's ILIT paragraph: research on ILIT for dog allergy is limited, and a small 2021 placebo controlled trial found no clear benefit for dog, cat or dust mite allergy ([Park 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8178859/)), or at least the house "promising but smaller, results vary" line. *Why:* YMYL accuracy. Right now the body promotes ILIT for dogs more strongly than the evidence supports, and the house rule elsewhere is to say this plainly. *Where:* `content-drafts/articles/published/allergic-to-my-dog.md`, "Longer term options for dog allergy", then republish. *Effort:* small.
2. **Use the new AI search tools in Search Console and Bing.** In Search Console, confirm Settings > Search generative AI is set to Include (the default; if anyone changed it, AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions stop). Then check the Search Generative AI performance report and the new multimodal filter once a month. In Bing Webmaster Tools, open AI Performance > Topics Portfolio and copy any "opportunity queries" into `docs/content-ideas.md`. *Why:* these are the first direct readouts of how OAAN shows up in AI answers, and AI search is a stated traffic goal. *Where:* the two consoles (an owner task). *Effort:* small.
3. **Give the house author a URL in the Article JSON-LD.** Change the Organization author to `{ "@type": "Organization", "@id": SITE_URL + "/#organization", name, url: SITE_URL }`, and add the same `@id` to the homepage Organization. *Why:* Google's Article docs recommend `author.url`, and the `@id` ties each article to the Organization entity (logo, sameAs) that AI and Search use to judge who wrote it. *Where:* `src/components/ArticleDetail.tsx:224`, `src/app/(site)/page.tsx:35`. *Effort:* small.
4. **Disclose AI generated images.** The puffy eyes hero is AI generated, and the page and the About disclosure say nothing about it. Add "and some illustrative images are AI generated" to the About page's "Checked before publishing" text, add a short caption or credit line on any article whose hero is AI generated, and add a content-pipeline rule for it. *Why:* Google's updated generative AI guidance (2026-10-01) says disclosure gives readers context and that image metadata can be added. A health site's trust signals are worth more than the effort this takes. *Where:* `src/app/(site)/about/page.tsx:226`, `content-drafts/articles/published/puffy-eyes-allergies.md`, `docs/content-pipeline.md`. *Effort:* small.
5. **Small cleanups.** Cut the "drops the t" sentence in the Kenalog article. Update `docs/metadata-rules.md` (the state page H1 at line 155, and re-run the "Last audited" title and description check over the 21 articles and the 10-30 practice titles). *Effort:* small.
