This Cookie Policy explains how Green in Blue Digital LLC, doing business as Open Air Allergy Network ("we," "us," or "our"), uses cookies on openairallergynetwork.com (the "Site").

Cookies are small data files stored on your device by a website. Cookies cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer. We use persistent cookies, which stay on your device until they expire or you delete them, to remember your choices, to understand how visitors use the Site, and to measure how well our ads perform.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following types of cookies:

Strictly necessary cookies

Our website requires these cookies to operate properly or to provide functions you request. They remember your privacy and cookie choices, and they protect the Site from bots and abuse through security checks run by our hosting provider. These are persistent cookies that last 1 year for your privacy choices and 1 hour for security checks.

Analytical/performance cookies

These cookies allow us and our third-party service providers to recognize and count the number of visitors and to see how visitors move around our website when they are using it. This helps us improve how our website works by, for example, ensuring that users can easily find what they need on our website. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it, including which pages are visited, how visitors found us, and which features they use. We see this information in aggregate and don't use it to identify individual visitors. These are persistent cookies that last up to 2 years.

Functionality cookies

These cookies enable helpful but non-essential website functions that improve your website experience. They remember choices you make on our Site, such as the zip code you last searched, so you don't have to enter them again. They don't identify you. These are persistent cookies that last 1 year.

Advertising and tracking cookies

These cookies measure how well our ads perform, such as which ad or campaign brought you to our Site. They may enable visitor identification across websites and over time. These are persistent cookies that last up to 90 days.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies on our Site are set by third parties, such as advertising networks and web traffic analysis services. These cookies may enable visitor identification across websites and over time. These third-party cookies are analytical or advertising cookies. We do not control the third party's use of those cookies, their duration, or their ability to share information with other third parties. Our advertising partners, such as Google, set cookies to measure how well our ads perform, such as which ads lead to visits to our Site. These partners' own privacy policies also apply.

Reviewing Our Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Policy describes our practices for any personal data that our cookies collect, store, or use.

Your Cookie Choices

You may turn off any cookies that are not strictly necessary at any time on our My Privacy Choices page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we automatically turn off advertising cookies.

Most web browsers also allow you to directly block all cookies, or just third-party cookies, through your browser settings. Using your browser settings to block all cookies, including strictly necessary ones, may interfere with proper site operation.
