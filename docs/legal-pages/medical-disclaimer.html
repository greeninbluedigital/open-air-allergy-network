About ILIT

Intralymphatic immunotherapy (ILIT) is a newer form of allergy immunotherapy. ILIT has been studied in clinical trials, but it has not been reviewed for approval by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). It isn't the right choice for everyone. Treatment protocols, number of visits, costs, and insurance coverage vary from practice to practice. Only a qualified healthcare professional who knows your medical history can tell you whether ILIT, or another treatment, is appropriate for you.
