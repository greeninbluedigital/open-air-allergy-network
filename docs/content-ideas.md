# Content ideas and published articles

One place for every article idea (Blog and Learn), plus a record of what's
been written. When an idea is published, move it to "Published" with its
date and URL. Writing rules: `OpenAirAllergyNetwork_STYLE_GUIDE.md` and
`docs/blog-content-guide.md`. Keyword volumes aren't verified; check them in
Semrush before choosing.

**Blog vs. Learn:** Blog (`/blog/...`) is for narrower, timely topics and
practice-contributed articles. Learn (`/learn-about-ilit/...`) is for broad,
evergreen, keyword-targeted guides.
