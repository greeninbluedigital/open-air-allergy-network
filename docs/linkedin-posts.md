# LinkedIn posts (OAAN company page)

Decided 2026-10-25. LinkedIn is for credibility with allergists, practice
staff, journalists and partners, not for SEO (LinkedIn links are nofollow).
About **two posts a month**, company page only. Never the owner's personal
profile: his network is the auto industry and stays that way.

A reminder task (`oaan-linkedin-post-reminder`, 1st and 15th of each month)
drafts the next post. Update this log when a post goes up or is scheduled.

## Rules for the post text

- Audience: allergists and practice staff. Measured, evidence-first tone.
  State ILIT's weak spots plainly (smaller, lower quality research base) next
  to its advantages. No hype, no consumer sales language.
- Every fact comes from the linked article's own sourced text. Nothing new.
- 2 short paragraphs plus the link. Up to three hashtags, e.g. #Immunotherapy
  #Allergy #ILIT.
- National authority framing: never mention which cities or regions OAAN
  covers.
- Style guide applies: no em dashes, no semicolons joining clauses.
