## Extended Bio (1948 characters)

Dr. Roua Azmeh leads Dayton Allergy & Asthma Care, an allergy and immunology practice at 5250 Far Hills Avenue in Dayton. According to the practice, she grew up in the Dayton area and earned her bachelor's degree at the University of Dayton and her medical degree at Washington University in St. Louis. She completed a pediatrics residency at the University of Michigan and an allergy and immunology fellowship at Saint Louis University, then spent several years on the faculty of the Western Michigan University allergy and immunology clinic before returning home to private practice. She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology (AAAAI) and the American College of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology (ACAAI).

The practice offers ILIT (intralymphatic immunotherapy), a faster alternative to allergy shots. ILIT places a small dose of allergen directly into a lymph node, and a course typically takes three injections about a month apart rather than years of regular shots. Whether ILIT fits a particular person's allergies is a decision for the patient and Dr. Azmeh to make together.

Its website lists the conditions it evaluates and treats: environmental allergies such as hay fever, pet allergies and dust allergy, nasal and sinus symptoms, asthma, eczema, hives and swelling, food, medication, metal and skin contact allergies, and immune deficiency. Services include allergy skin testing for environmental allergens, foods and select medications, allergy shots, patch testing, oral food and medication challenges, and spirometry, a breathing test for asthma.

Dr. Azmeh says she takes the time to hear each patient's concerns and explain their condition, and she uses evidence-based medicine and shared decision-making to build a treatment plan. For anyone looking for an allergist in Dayton, allergy testing in Dayton or a faster option than years of allergy shots, the office is in Suite 260 at 5250 Far Hills Avenue.
