### AA Extended Bio (1780 characters)

Allergenix is a direct specialty allergy and immunology clinic in West Des Moines, serving Clive, Waukee, Urbandale and patients from across Iowa. New patient visits run 45 to 60 minutes, appointments are usually available the same week, and one physician, Dr. Sean Stanga, handles your evaluation, testing and treatment plan from start to finish. Telehealth visits are available too.

Allergy testing at Allergenix is built around getting answers quickly. Skin testing is read during the visit, blood results come back in one to two days, and every patient leaves with a written plan. The clinic treats seasonal and year-round allergies, food allergies (including supervised oral food challenges), asthma (with lung function testing and biologic therapy), eczema, chronic hives and long-running sinus and nasal symptoms. It also offers penicillin allergy testing for patients carrying an old antibiotic allergy label.

For environmental allergies to pollen, dust mites, pet dander or mold, Allergenix offers RapImmune®, its name for intralymphatic immunotherapy (ILIT). Small doses of allergen are placed into a lymph node under ultrasound guidance in three visits over about eight weeks, compared with years of weekly and monthly allergy shots. The clinic also offers sublingual allergy drops, food sublingual immunotherapy and venom immunotherapy.

Allergenix is a direct-pay practice with published prices, no insurance billing, and HSA, FSA and CareCredit accepted.

## FAQs tab (Provider Slug: allergenix-west-des-moines-ia)

**1. Does Allergenix offer a faster alternative to allergy shots?**
Yes. RapImmune®, the clinic's name for intralymphatic immunotherapy (ILIT), is given in three ultrasound-guided visits over about eight weeks. It treats environmental allergies to pollen, dust mites, pet dander and mold. Food and stinging insect allergies are handled through the clinic's separate programs.

**2. How much does RapImmune cost at Allergenix?**
Allergenix publishes a one-time flat fee of $3,500 for RapImmune. It covers the consultation, lymph node mapping, ultrasound guidance, boosters when needed and all related follow-up visits. Financing is available, and HSA, FSA and CareCredit are accepted.

**3. Does Allergenix take insurance?**
No. Allergenix is a direct specialty care clinic, so patients pay the clinic directly at published prices instead of going through insurance. Many patients use an HSA or FSA, and CareCredit is accepted.

**4. How soon can I see an allergist in West Des Moines?**
Allergenix usually has appointments the same week. A first visit runs 45 to 60 minutes, and telehealth consultations are available if you'd rather start from home.

**5. Will I get my allergy test results the same day?**
Skin test results are read during your visit, and you leave with a written treatment plan. If you need blood testing instead, results typically come back in one to two days.

**6. Does Allergenix treat children?**
Yes. Dr. Stanga is board certified in both allergy and immunology and pediatrics. Whether a child is ready for RapImmune depends on the child's maturity, and he'll talk that through with your family.

**7. Who isn't a good fit for RapImmune?**
The clinic doesn't recommend it during pregnancy, with uncontrolled asthma, or for patients taking a beta blocker without a plan to address it. It's also not used when testing doesn't show a clear environmental allergy that matches your symptoms.

**8. Can Allergenix help with food allergies?**
Yes. The clinic offers full food allergy testing, supervised oral food challenges that take four to five hours, food sublingual immunotherapy and guidance on safely reintroducing foods.

**9. Where is Allergenix, and what are its hours?**
The clinic is at 595 S 60th St, Suite 160, in West Des Moines, convenient to Clive, Waukee and Urbandale. It's open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.
