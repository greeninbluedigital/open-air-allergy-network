# Keyword updates for the six older articles (for review)

Drafted 2026-10-05 for the new keyword strategy (docs/blog-content-guide.md,
"Keyword strategy"). No article bodies are rewritten. Changes are limited to
Google descriptions, Key Takeaways, FAQ questions and a few headings, and
every claim is one the article already makes. Nothing is live until you
approve it.

Why the descriptions matter most: five of the six have **no Google
description**, so Google currently builds one from the Key Takeaways.
A written one lets us put the search terms exactly where searchers see them.

---

## 1. Allergy Shots vs. Allergy Drops vs. ILIT (Learn)

**Google description (new, 139 chars)**
> Allergy shots vs. allergy drops vs. ILIT: how each works, how long each takes, and which is the fastest allergy treatment course to finish.

**Key Takeaway 3**
- Now: ILIT delivers allergen into a lymph node instead, typically over a handful of visits across a few months.
- New: ILIT, the fastest of the three to finish, delivers allergen into a lymph node, typically over a handful of visits across a few months.

**FAQ 2 question and first words**
- Now: "How is ILIT different from allergy shots and drops?"
- New: "Is ILIT a faster allergy treatment than shots or drops?" The answer gains
  an opening "Yes, in how long the course takes." The rest stays as is.

**Optional title change** (61 chars): "Allergy Shots vs. Drops vs. ILIT: Which Treatment Is Fastest?"
Stronger for "fast" searches, but it drops the exact phrase "allergy drops" from
the title. My lean: keep the current title, since the description and FAQ now
carry "fastest".
